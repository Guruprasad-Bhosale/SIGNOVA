## 2. In-Depth Dataset Findings

### 2.1 `ISLTranslate` (ACL 2023 Finding)
- **Source**: Exploration-Lab / iSign (Hugging Face dataset `Exploration-Lab/iSign`).
- **Structure**: 
  - `data/ISLTranslate.csv`: Contains 31,224 rows (31,222 usable sentence records). Columns: `uid`, `text`.
  - `data/ISL-signer_validation.csv`: Contains 291 gold standard samples verified by a certified ISL signer.
- **Continuous vs. Isolated**: **Continuous ISL sentences and phrases**.
- **Signer Disambiguation**: The `uid` column follows a prefix convention (`{signer_hash}-{sentence_id}`), allowing signer-independent evaluation splits.
- **Duplicate Handling**: Detected 2 duplicate UID keys in the raw CSV (`UbhONRGm-90--61`). SIGNOVA's adapter automatically disambiguates duplicate keys with suffix tags (`_dup1`).

### 2.2 `INCLUDE` Dataset (Zenodo 4010759)
- **Source**: Zenodo Record ID 4010759.
- **Structure**: 263 isolated word-level signs across 4,287 video clips.
- **Use in SIGNOVA**: Excellent candidate for initial pre-training of the spatial landmark encoder before fine-tuning on continuous sentence data.
